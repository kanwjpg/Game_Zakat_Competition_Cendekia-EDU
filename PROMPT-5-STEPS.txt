Build the shell of an HTML5 educational game about zakat for students aged 13-18, in
English. ONE `index.html`, all HTML/CSS/JS inline. No build step, no frameworks, no
libraries, no image or audio files. Must run by opening the file, offline.

Title: **Zakat Quest — The Path of Prosperity**

This prompt builds everything EXCEPT chapter content, the quiz, and the calculator. Leave
`const CHAPTERS = []` as an empty array with a comment saying content arrives later. The
file must already open and look finished.

**Constraints.** Plain JS, no modules. Only outbound request: Google Fonts (Baloo 2 + Plus
Jakarta Sans) with system fallback. All art is inline SVG you write. All sound synthesised
with Web Audio. Progress in localStorage, every access in try/catch. Fixed viewport, no page
scroll — panels scroll internally. `100dvh` not `100vh`. Works 390px to 1440px.

**Palette** (CSS custom properties on `:root`, used everywhere):
```
--green #0e6b46  --green-dark #0a5238  --green-deep #063b28  --green-pale #dcefe4
--gold  #d4a94e  --gold-dark  #9a7726  --gold-pale  #f6e7bc
--cream #fdf8ee  --ink #10352a  --red #d9534f (wrong answers only)
```

**Look** — deep Islamic green and gold, styled like an Indonesian Ramadan calendar poster.
Flat fills, no component gradients, soft green-tinted shadows on cards only.
- Background: radial green gradient + thin gold calendar grid + oversized numerals 1-8
  sitting in the cells at ~10% opacity, like dates on a poster.
- Three gold lanterns hanging on chains from the top, swaying, different chain lengths.
  A crescent and star in one corner. Faint eight-point star (girih) tiling.
- Fixed `pointer-events:none` paper-grain overlay (inline SVG feTurbulence, ~5% opacity).
- White cards with soft shadow, reading as pinned paper. Arabesque divider ornaments.
- Radius: pill for controls, 26px for cards, 18px inside cards.
- Display type `letter-spacing:-.02em` + `text-wrap:balance`. Prose max ~65ch.
  Every changing number gets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

**Build these screens.**
- A screen router. Transitions use an eight-point-star iris wipe (full-screen element with a
  star `clip-path`, scaling up then down). Custom cubic-bezier easing, never browser default.
- **Title:** emblem with a slowly rotating girih ring, subtitle, arabesque divider, fact
  chips reading "8 chapters · 52 questions · 8 mini-games · 7 zakat calculators", one-line

- **Map:** green calendar panel with gold grid and chapter numerals in the cells, date palm

  eight placeholder markers so the screen can be seen.
- **Settings:** sound on/off, ambient music on/off, animation Full / Light / Off, larger
  text, high contrast — all saved to localStorage. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **How to Play** and empty **Calculator** and **Progress** screens, reachable and styled.

**Sound engine.** Oscillators only, tuned to maqam Hijaz (D E♭ F♯ G A B♭ C): click, a rising
three-note figure for correct, falling for wrong, a flourish for finishing. Plus a quiet
ambient drone and slow melody from the same scale, low-pass filtered, started on the first
tap because browsers block audio before a gesture, with its own switch.

**Traps:** add `[hidden]{display:none !important}` — a CSS `display` rule silently defeats
the attribute. Never white text on a white surface, including ghost buttons on white bars.
Nothing clipped at 390px. No console errors.

Write the complete file. No placeholders, no `// ...`.

║  PROMPT 2 of 5 — GAME ENGINES AND CHAPTERS 1-2                               ║

Keep everything already built. Add the playable loop and the first two chapters.

**Chapter flow:** Story → Quiz → Mini-game → Results → back to Map.

**Story screen.** Full-bleed SVG backdrop, dialogue box over it, text typing out character by
character, a tap completes it instantly. One dot per line, a Skip button, named speakers with

**Quiz screen.** Four options A-D, shuffled each time. Three hearts, a countdown timer bar, a
live score. A Hint button costs 30 points and removes one wrong option. After every answer an
explanation panel opens, with a scriptural source where one applies.
Score = 100 base + up to 60 for speed + 15 per consecutive correct answer, capped at 75.
Losing all three hearts ends the chapter.
Keyboard: A-D or 1-4 answer, H hint, Enter continue, Esc close.
**Disable the Next button for ~300ms after answering**, or the same Enter press that submits
an answer also skips the explanation.

**Three mini-game engines, each driven by data:**
- **Sort** — cards into labelled baskets. Drag-and-drop *and* tap-card-then-tap-basket,
  because drag does not work on phones. 60 points per correct card.
- **Scale** — a balance beam where **the heavier side drops**; the player judges "Zakat is
  due" or "Not yet due" and gets an explanation. 80 points.
- **Calc** — a number pad, also usable from the keyboard. 90 points.

**Results screen.** Three stars (3 flawless, 2 for one miss, 1 otherwise), the score counting
up, an arabesque divider, and a stat grid: correct, wrong, accuracy, best streak, time,
mini-game bonus. Buttons for next chapter, replay, map. Save stars and best score per chapter.

**Now write chapters 1 and 2 in full** — story dialogue, 6 questions each with four options,
a hint, an explanation and a source where relevant, one mini-game, and a distinct flat
animated SVG backdrop. These facts must be exact:

**1. Gate of Knowledge** — zakat means grow/purify/bless; it is the **3rd pillar** of Islam;
zakat is obligatory while infaq and sadaqah are voluntary; muzakki gives, mustahik receives;
it is fard 'ayn. Sources: Qur'an 2:43, Qur'an 9:103.
Mini-game: sort cards into Obligatory versus Voluntary.

**2. Barokah Market** — nisab is **85g of gold** or **595g of silver**; the rate is **2.5%**;
**haul is one lunar year** of full ownership; cash and savings follow the gold nisab.
Conditions: full ownership, growth potential, reaching nisab, above basic needs, free of debt
falling due, haul complete.
Mini-game: the nisab scale with five cases, including one below nisab and one where the haul
is not yet complete.

**3. Green Fields** — crops, livestock, rikaz.
Crop nisab **5 wasaq ≈ 653kg** of dried grain. **10%** where watering costs nothing (rain,
spring), **5%** where irrigation is paid for. Due **at every harvest, no haul**.
Livestock nisab: camels 5, cattle 30, sheep and goats 40.
**Rikaz** (buried or mined wealth) is **20%**, immediately, with no nisab and no haul.

**4. The Amil's Office** — income and trade.
Income zakat rests on **MUI Fatwa No. 3 of 2003**, nisab **85g of gold per year**, rate

Trade: **(working capital + profit + collectible receivables) − (debt falling due + losses),
× 2.5%**, inventory valued at selling price when the haul completes.
Mini-game: calculation.

**5. Asnaf Village** — the **eight recipients, Qur'an 9:60**: fakir (no wealth or income),
miskin (income that does not suffice), amil (administrators), muallaf (new Muslims, hearts
reconciled), riqab (freeing from bondage), gharim (crushed by lawful debt), fi sabilillah
(in the path of Allah), ibn sabil (stranded traveller).
Not entitled: the wealthy, the able-bodied who can earn, and the parents, children and spouse
the giver already supports.
Mini-game: sort villagers into Fakir/Miskin, Gharim, Ibn Sabil, and Not Entitled.

**6. Night of Takbir** — zakat al-fitr.
**One sa' ≈ 2.5kg or 3.5 litres** of the local staple food **per person**. Due from every
Muslim soul, including a baby born before sunset on the last day of Ramadan. Permitted from
early Ramadan, obligatory at sunset on the final day, **best before the Eid prayer**; paid
after that prayer it counts as ordinary sadaqah. It purifies the fast from idle talk and
feeds the poor on the day of Eid.
Mini-game: sort payment moments into Best, Permitted, and Too Late.

**7. World of Giving** — zakat as a global instrument. This chapter carries the competition
theme, so give it weight. Teach:
- **naql al-zakat** — sending zakat abroad is generally permitted; local need normally comes
  first, but many scholars prefer sending it where need is far more severe.
- Refugees and displaced families qualify as **fakir, miskin and ibn sabil**, because the
  categories are defined by **circumstance, not nationality**.
- Zakat is **obligatory and annual**, so its flow is predictable enough to fund multi-year
  programmes — unlike emergency appeals, which rise and fade with attention.
- It maps onto the **Sustainable Development Goals**: 1 no poverty, 2 zero hunger, 3 health,
  4 education, 6 clean water, 8 decent work.
- **UNHCR's Refugee Zakat Fund**, launched 2019.
- On the size of global zakat: say **hundreds of billions of US dollars a year, with
  estimates varying widely**. Never state one figure as fact — the methods genuinely differ.

Backdrop: a flat globe with dotted flight arcs between points, and four labelled aid icons
around it — CLEAN WATER, EDUCATION, HEALTH CARE, FOOD RELIEF.
Mini-game: sort real zakat programmes under the goals they serve.

**8. Final Test** — ten mixed questions drawing on every chapter, including arithmetic and
two on chapter 7. Shorter clock, **no hints**. Mini-game: four closing calculations.

**Numbers — get this right.** English convention: comma for thousands, dot for decimals. Add

**3D gold coin** — CSS transforms only, no library, no model files. A spinning coin bursts

carries `perspective`; the inner element spins. Animate transform and opacity only.
It obeys the animation setting: Full spins it, Light and Off do not spawn it.
