Build a complete HTML5 educational game about zakat for students aged 13-18, in English.
Deliver ONE `index.html` with all HTML, CSS and JS inline. No build step, no frameworks, no
libraries, no image or audio files. It must run by opening the file, offline.

Title: **Zakat Quest — The Path of Prosperity**

## Constraints
- Plain JS, no modules. Only outbound request allowed: Google Fonts (Baloo 2 + Plus Jakarta
  Sans), with system fonts as fallback.
- All art is inline SVG you write. All sound synthesised with Web Audio. No asset files.
- Progress in localStorage, every access in try/catch, game still runs if storage fails.
- Fixed viewport, no page scroll — panels scroll internally. `100dvh`, not `100vh`.
  Works 390px to 1440px.

## Look — deep Islamic green and gold, styled like an Indonesian Ramadan calendar poster
Flat fills, no component gradients, soft green-tinted shadows on cards only.

```
--green #0e6b46  --green-dark #0a5238  --green-deep #063b28  --green-pale #dcefe4
--gold  #d4a94e  --gold-dark  #9a7726  --gold-pale  #f6e7bc
--cream #fdf8ee  --ink #10352a  --red #d9534f (wrong answers only)
```

- Background: radial green gradient + thin gold calendar grid + oversized numerals 1-8
  sitting in the cells at ~10% opacity, like dates on a poster.
- Three gold lanterns hanging on chains from the top, swaying, different chain lengths.
  A crescent and star in one corner. Faint eight-point star (girih) tiling.
- Fixed `pointer-events:none` paper-grain overlay (inline SVG feTurbulence, ~5% opacity).
- White cards with soft shadow, reading as pinned paper. Arabesque divider ornaments.
- Radius rule: pill for controls, 26px for cards, 18px inside cards.
- Display type: `letter-spacing:-.02em` + `text-wrap:balance`. Prose max ~65ch.
  Every changing number uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## 3D coin (CSS transforms only — no Three.js, no .glb)
A spinning gold coin bursts from the chosen option on every correct answer.
Two circular faces at `translateZ(±t/2)`, plus a rim of ~12 short segments, each placed at
**`rotateZ(i*30deg) translateY(-r) rotateX(90deg)`** and only as tall as the coin is thick.
Using `rotateY + translateZ` instead builds a standing drum, not a coin — that is the trap.
Shade each segment by the angle it faces: `brightness(0.70 + 0.45*cos(angle))`.
Spin as `rotateX(-18deg) rotateY(360deg)` so the thickness stays visible.
Outer element flies and holds `perspective`; inner element spins. Transform/opacity only.

## Flow
Title → Map → per chapter (Story → Quiz → Mini-game → Results) → Map.
Plus Calculator and Progress screens. Screen transitions: an eight-point-star iris wipe.

- **Title:** emblem with rotating girih ring, subtitle, arabesque divider, fact chips
  ("8 chapters · 52 questions · 8 mini-games · 7 zakat calculators"), 5 buttons.
- **Map:** green calendar panel, gold grid, 8 markers on a zig-zag gold path — solid behind,
  dotted ahead. Locked padlock, red ring on current, stars under each. Ch.1 always open.
- **Story:** full-bleed SVG backdrop, dialogue box, text types out, tap to complete, Skip.
- **Quiz:** 4 options A-D shuffled, 3 hearts, timer bar, live score. Hint costs 30 points and
  removes a wrong option (no hints in ch.8). Explanation panel after every answer.
  Score = 100 + up to 60 for speed + 15 per streak (max 75). No lives left ends the chapter.
- **Results:** 3 stars (3 = flawless, 2 = one miss), score counts up, stat grid.
- **Keyboard:** A-D or 1-4 answer, H hint, Enter continue, Esc close.

## Mini-games — three data-driven engines
- **Sort:** cards into labelled baskets. Drag-and-drop *and* tap-card-then-tap-basket,
  because drag does not work on phones. 60 pts per correct card.
- **Scale:** a balance beam where **the heavier side drops**; judge "due" or "not yet due".
  80 pts.
- **Calc:** number pad, also keyboard-usable. 90 pts.

## Calculator — 7 tabs, live results, breakdown and verdict
Income (monthly, Gross or Net — Net deducts basic needs, field disabled under Gross; compare
to 85g gold ÷ 12) · Gold & Silver (85g / 595g separately) · Savings & Cash (minus debt due) ·
Trade (ch.4 formula) · Crops (kg × 10% or 5%) · Zakat al-Fitr (people × 2.5kg, food or cash) ·
Rikaz (× 20%).

Collapsible "Reference prices" panel: editable **currency label** (Rp, $, RM…) plus gold,
silver and staple-food prices, all saved. Say plainly that the shipped figures are examples
and must be updated.

**Numbers:** English convention — comma thousands, dot decimal. Add separators *while typing*
but only when the caret is at the end. Write the parser and formatter as an exact pair, so
anything formatted reads back identical. Do not guess whether a dot is a separator.

## Progress, settings, sound
24 stars (3 per chapter), points, 6 ranks Beginner → Zakat Expert, 9 badges, local top-10
scoreboard, accuracy stats. Settings: sound, ambient music, **animation Full / Light / Off**,
larger text, high contrast — all saved. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Visible focus
rings. Ask the player's name on first Start, not on load.

Sound: oscillators only, tuned to **maqam Hijaz** (D E♭ F♯ G A B♭ C) — clicks, a rising
three-note figure for correct, falling for wrong, a flourish on finishing a chapter. Plus a
quiet ambient drone and slow melody from the same scale, low-pass filtered, started on the
first tap (browsers block audio before a gesture), with its own switch.

## Traps to avoid — check each before you finish
- `[hidden]{display:none !important}` — a CSS `display` rule silently defeats the attribute.
- Never white text on a white surface. Check every button, including ghost buttons on
  white bars.
- Disable the Next button for ~300ms after answering, or the same Enter press that submits
  also skips the explanation.
- Typing `18000000` must show `18,000,000`, not something mangled.
- The balance beam: heavier side drops.
- Nothing clipped at 390px. No console errors.

Write the complete file. No abbreviation, no `// ...` placeholders, no skeleton — all eight
chapters and all their questions written out in full.
